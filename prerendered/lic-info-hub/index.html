    Insurance Abbreviations & Codes

    Quick reference for LIC terminology, internal codes, private insurers, and common policy terms

    General LIC AbbreviationsLIC Internal CodesPrivate InsurersCommon Policy Terms

    General LIC Abbreviations(30)

    LIC

    General

    Life Insurance Corporation

    India's largest government-owned life insurance company

    DOC

    General

    Date of Commencement

    Start date of the insurance policy

    DLP

    General

    Date of Last Premium

    Date when the last premium was paid

    DOM

    General

    Date of Maturity

    Date when the policy matures

    DOD

    General

    Date of Death

    Date on which the life assured dies

    Maturity Date

    General

    Date when the policy term ends and benefits are payable

    SA

    General

    Sum Assured

    Guaranteed amount payable on death/maturity

    PA

    General

    Proposer/Policyholder Address

    The address of the person who owns the policy

    LA

    General

    Life Assured

    The person whose life is insured

    DOB

    General

    Date of Birth

    Used for age calculation and underwriting

    FUP

    General

    First Unpaid Premium

    The first missed premium date

    LUP

    General

    Last Unpaid Premium

    The final unpaid premium before policy lapses

    HPR

    General

    History of Premium Receipt

    Record of paid premiums

    NAV

    General

    Net Asset Value

    Used in ULIPs for fund value calculation

    ULIP

    General

    Unit Linked Insurance Plan

    A mix of insurance and investment

    NACH

    General

    National Automated Clearing House

    System for auto-debiting premiums from bank accounts

    NEFT

    General

    National Electronic Funds Transfer

    For direct credit of claim/maturity to policyholder's account

    ECS

    General

    Electronic Clearing System

    Used for auto payment of premiums

    TPA

    General

    Third Party Administrator

    Handles claims in health insurance

    GST

    General

    Goods and Services Tax

    Tax levied on insurance premiums

    IRDAI

    General

    Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India

    Regulatory body for insurance in India

    TDS

    General

    Tax Deducted at Source

    Tax deducted on certain payouts like commissions or maturity benefits

    KYC

    General

    Know Your Customer

    Verification process of identity and address

    PAN

    General

    Permanent Account Number

    Used for tax and financial transactions

    GSV

    General

    Guaranteed Surrender Value

    The minimum surrender value the insurer must pay, fixed as a percentage of premiums paid

    SSV

    General

    Special Surrender Value

    A discretionary surrender value that may be higher than the GSV, based on accrued bonuses

    WOP

    General

    Waiver of Premium

    A rider that waives future premiums if the policyholder is disabled or diagnosed with a critical illness

    ADB

    General

    Accidental Death Benefit

    An additional payout if death occurs due to an accident

    UIN

    General

    Unique Identification Number

    The IRDAI-issued number that uniquely identifies an approved insurance product

    CKYC

    General

    Central KYC Registry

    A centralized system that stores a customer's KYC records for reuse across financial institutions

    Common Policy Terms(13)

    Grace Period

    Common

    The window after a missed premium due date — typically 15 or 30 days — during which the policy stays active and the premium can still be paid without penalty

    Free Look Period

    Common

    A window (commonly 15 or 30 days) after receiving the policy document during which the policyholder can review and return the policy for a refund if unsatisfied

    Paid-up Value

    Common

    A reduced sum assured a policy converts to if premiums stop after a minimum number of years, instead of lapsing entirely

    Nomination

    Common

    Naming a person to receive the policy proceeds on the life assured's death — the nominee acts as a trustee for the legal heirs unless also a beneficial owner

    Assignment

    Common

    Legally transferring the ownership rights of a policy to another person or entity, often used when a policy is pledged for a loan

    Rider

    Common

    An optional add-on benefit attached to a base policy for extra premium, such as critical illness or accidental death cover

    Lapse

    Common

    When a policy loses its benefits because a premium wasn't paid even after the grace period

    Revival

    Common

    Reinstating a lapsed policy by paying overdue premiums plus interest, usually within a specified revival period

    IDV

    Common

    Insured Declared Value

    In motor insurance, the current market value of the vehicle — the maximum amount payable in case of total loss or theft

    NCB

    Common

    No Claim Bonus

    A discount on motor or health premium for each claim-free year, which resets if a claim is made

    Cashless Claim

    Common

    A health insurance claim settled directly between the insurer and a network hospital, without the policyholder paying upfront

    Waiting Period

    Common

    A defined period after policy start during which certain health conditions or claims are not covered

    Family Floater

    Common

    A single health insurance sum assured shared across all covered family members, rather than a separate cover for each
